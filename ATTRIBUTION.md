# Attribution

Every background in this project is derived from an image shipped with
[Omarchy](https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy) (MIT). Omarchy's themes are
community and first-party contributions, and the artwork within them belongs to
its original creators — this project claims no ownership of any of it.

## What was done to each image

Each file was converted to 5120x1440 by one of:

- **crop** — a 32:9 band cut from the full-resolution original. No pixels invented.
- **outpaint** — the flanks generated with Flux.1 Fill (local) or Flux Pro (BFL API);
  the original is composited back over the centre unchanged.
- **pixel-mirror** — the image's own edge pixels mirrored outward and repainted
  enough to break the symmetry.
- **matte** — the flanks filled with a colour field, an edge gradient, or a fade to
  black. Nothing invented.

The 21:9 packs are straight crops of the 32:9 masters.

## Requests to remove

If your work appears here and you would prefer it did not, open an issue or email
the maintainer; it will be removed the same day, no questions asked.
